## The work

Three hours, and it is writing.

### 1 · The spine

Four sections, in this order, and the order is the lesson:

```
1  what you must act on          blockers, each with the evidence and the cost of inaction
2  what this evaluation cannot see
3  the numbers                   each with n and the MDE of its set
4  what expires                  durable / re-measure annually / already expired
```

Section 2 before section 3. A report that puts its limitations last is a report whose
limitations were not read.

### 2 · The MDE audit

Walk back through your weeks 2 to 11 reports and mark every claimed delta **above** or
**below** the minimum detectable effect of the set it was measured on.

Week 9 built this table. Week 11 added a row for your own agent. Today it is one table,
once, with the numbers.

### 3 · Strip the decorations

Every bare number in your draft is a decoration. `0.750` becomes `0.750 (n=20, MDE 0.1003)`
or it comes out.

## Deep track

> Find a published RAG evaluation — a vendor benchmark, a model card, an arXiv table — and
> apply the week-9 arithmetic to it. Sample size is usually reported; standard deviation
> almost never is. Write down what you would need to ask for, and how many of its claims
> you can assess without it. The answer is usually none, and that is a finding about the
> literature rather than about the paper.
