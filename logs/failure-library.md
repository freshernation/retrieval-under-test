# The failure library

One card per failure, added as you meet them. Roughly thirty by week 12.

This is the thing you carry out of this course. Not the code, not the numbers — this.

---

## Card template

### F-000 — [short name for the failure]

**Station** — [1-7]
**Symptom** — [what you saw, in the words you would have used before you knew the cause]
**Diagnostic** — [what you looked at that identified it, specifically enough to repeat]
**Fix** — [what you changed]
**Delta** — [metric before → after (run:id vs run:id), n, interval]
**Stops working when** — [the conditions under which this fix is the wrong one]
