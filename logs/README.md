# Logs

Three files. Two are read by your instructor before every live hour and neither is marked.
An honest amber gets you help; a blank log gets you asked why it is blank.

| File | What goes in it | Who reads it |
|---|---|---|
| `stuck-log.md` | What stalled you, and for how long | your instructor, before each live hour |
| `signal-log.md` | Five numbers a day, and every `SIGNAL` block from an AI role | your instructor |
| `failure-library.md` | The thing you carry out of this course | you, for years |

The failure library is not a log. It is the portfolio artifact — roughly thirty one-page
cards by week 12 — and it is the reason you will be able to debug a retrieval system on a
corpus nobody has written about.
